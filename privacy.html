Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Drawbly is an iPad app that teaches you to draw cute things one step at a time. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain words.

What Drawbly collects

Drawbly does not have accounts, does not ask for your name or email, and does not run analytics or advertising trackers.

When you ask for a drawing lesson, the subject you picked or typed (for example "a pug in a teacup") is sent to the Drawbly server, which asks an AI model to write the step-by-step lesson. That text is the only information that leaves your iPad. It is not linked to you, and Drawbly does not store it beyond what is needed to answer the request.

Drawbly uses Anthropic's Claude API to write lessons. The subject text is sent to Anthropic under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not allow it to be used to train their models.

What stays on your iPad

Your saved lessons, anything you draw in Draw here mode, and your settings are stored only on your iPad. Deleting the app deletes them. Drawbly never uploads your drawings.

Purchases

Paid features are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; Drawbly never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

Drawbly is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
